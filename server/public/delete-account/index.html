Delete Your Account — VerifiedPhone (Dealer Sathi)

Last updated: 20 October 2026

VerifiedPhone — Dealer Sathi is operated by The Allways Pvt. Ltd., Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. This page explains how to delete your shop account, or request deletion of a specific record, and what happens to your data when you do.

How to request deletion

VerifiedPhone does not yet have a self-service delete button in the app. To request deletion, email us from the address registered to your account (or state your registered mobile number) at:

info@theallwaysdigital.com

Include in your email:

  1. Your shop's registered mobile number
  2. Whether you want your entire shop account deleted, or only a specific seller/buyer record removed

We verify the request against the account, then action it within 30 days, and confirm by email once done.

What gets deleted

  • Full account deletion: your shop profile (name, GST number, address, contact details, logo) and your login are permanently removed.
  • Specific record deletion: the named seller or buyer's name, photo, Aadhaar images, and contact details on that one transaction record are removed.

What's retained, and for how long

Device purchase/sale transaction records (excluding the seller/buyer's personal identification fields, once a deletion request for that record is honored) are kept for up to 5 years from the transaction date, for fraud-prevention and record-keeping purposes consistent with common practice in the second-hand goods trade — see our Privacy Policy for details. Deleting your account does not retroactively delete transaction history tied to devices already recorded, except for the seller/buyer personal fields described above, which are removed on request.
