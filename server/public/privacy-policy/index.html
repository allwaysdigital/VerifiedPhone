Privacy Policy — VerifiedPhone (Dealer Sathi)

Last updated: 20 October 2026

VerifiedPhone — Dealer Sathi ("the App", "we", "us") is operated by The Allways Pvt. Ltd., Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

1. Information we collect

From the shop owner (the App's registered user)

  • Mobile number (used to log in via one-time password)
  • Shop name, GST number, business address, contact number
  • Shop logo (optional upload)

From sellers and buyers of devices

When a shop owner records a purchase or sale in the App, they enter:

  • Full name, mobile number, address, city, state, and pincode
  • A photograph of the person
  • A photograph of their Aadhaar card (front and back)

This data is entered by the shop owner, not directly by the seller or buyer. The shop owner is responsible for obtaining the seller's/ buyer's consent to record and store this information as part of their own transaction records.

Device information

Brand, model, IMEI number(s), condition, storage, color, battery health, purchase price, sale price, and photographs of the device itself.

2. How we use this information

  • To let a shop owner log in and manage their own inventory
  • To generate invoices for completed sales
  • To maintain a seller/buyer identification record for each device, as a fraud-prevention measure in the second-hand phone trade
  • To show the shop owner reports and analytics about their own business

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. Data storage and security

All data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption. Photographs and documents are stored in a private cloud storage bucket not directly browsable by the public. Access to a shop's data is restricted to that shop's own logged-in account.

5. Data retention

Seller/buyer identification records and device transaction records (including Aadhaar photographs) are retained for 5 years from the date of the transaction, for fraud-prevention and record-keeping purposes consistent with common practice in the second-hand goods trade. Shop account data (shop name, GST number, contact details) is retained for as long as the shop's account remains active.

6. Your rights

A shop owner may request export or deletion of their account's data by contacting us at the email below. A seller or buyer whose identification was recorded by a shop may also request deletion of their record by contacting us; we will verify the request and coordinate with the relevant shop before acting on it. The App does not currently have a self-service deletion flow — all requests are handled manually.

7. Children's privacy

This App is intended for business use by mobile phone dealers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
